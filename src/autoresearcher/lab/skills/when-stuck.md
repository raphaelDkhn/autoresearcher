# When stuck

Use this after 5 discards in a row, or when the backlog in notes.md is empty.

1. Re-read the harness for structure you have not used: what it rewards, what it ignores,
   and where the time of a run goes.
2. Profile a run. A floor is often an engineering limit, not a law of the problem.
3. Combine near-misses: discards that came within twice `min_improvement` of the best.
4. Test a direction your instinct says is wrong: the other sign of a knob you have only moved
   one way, or a family of ideas you have not tried.
5. Spend one run on a radically different approach. Never declare convergence.

Write what you learn in notes.md and refill the backlog before the next run.
